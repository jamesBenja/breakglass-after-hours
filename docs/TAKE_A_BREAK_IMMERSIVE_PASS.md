# Take A Break immersive pass

This pass intentionally turns the open west side of Take A Break into a dimensional breach instead of repairing it as ordinary wall geometry.

- The three lounge couches face inward toward the room.
- The house DJ faces into the booth and dance floor.
- Sam smoothly turns toward the local player while they move through the alley.
- The open wall reveals an animated star field, layered nebulae, psychedelic rings and luminous architectural edges.
- The Take A Break installation uses eight HRTF virtual speaker positions with circulating, non-repeating movement.
- While the player is in Take A Break, club music is reduced to quiet, heavily low-passed wall bleed while the installation has an independent output path and dominates the sound field.

The existing room-to-room audio engine and house-DJ transport remain intact. The new installation is additive and localized to Take A Break.
